A well-run immersion baptism comes down to preparation, not improvisation. Here is the full sequence, from filling the pool to drying off, for a church holding a service for the first time or looking to make its routine more reliable.

## One week ahead: preparation

- **Confirm the candidates** and brief them: bring a change of clothing (dark clothes that don't turn see-through when wet), walk through the gesture, note that the time under water is very brief.
- **Choose the location**: a flat, firm floor, near a water point for filling and a drain for emptying, with a dedicated power socket if the water needs heating on site.
- **Check the equipment**: pool structure, drain valve, fill hose, towels, robes, and a space to change.

## On the day: setup and filling

- **Set up the pool.** The Kollder portable baptistry unfolds and sets up in **under 2 minutes**, by one person, with no tools.
- **Fill it.** Plan for **130 to 185 gallons** for a 205 × 80 × 60 cm (81 × 31 × 24 in) pool, roughly 15 to 30 minutes on a garden hose depending on flow, or the equivalent in containers where no water point is nearby.
- **Set the level and temperature.** A water depth of **20 to 24 in (50 to 60 cm)** covers an adult lying down. Aim for water around **86°F (30°C)**, at room temperature or warmed beforehand, with none of the cold-water requirement of a medical tub.

## Just before the service

- **Brief the officiant and the assistant**: agree on the hold (one hand behind the upper back, one on the forearm) and the signal that starts the immersion.
- **Make the surroundings safe**: a non-slip mat at the entry and exit, towels and robes within reach, a warm space to change.
- **Let the candidate step in** and get used to the water for a moment before the gesture.

## The baptism itself

- The candidate stands or sits in the center of the pool, depending on mobility. The officiant stands alongside, in the water.
- After the words of the service, the officiant **lowers the candidate backward** into a full, brief immersion, head included, then **raises them smoothly**.
- A pause, then support to the edge of the pool.

## After the immersion

- Towel, robe, a warm space. Have a dedicated helper for each candidate at the exit.
- **Between candidates**, the same water stays usable for the whole session.
- **After the service**: drain through the valve, rinse with clean water, dry the structure before folding it into its carry bag.
