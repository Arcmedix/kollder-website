The Kollder portable baptistry uses the same stainless steel structure as Kollder One™ equipment, originally engineered for demanding emergency medical use, put to a far more peaceful purpose. Here's what that means in practice for a church.

## Setup built for one person

No church needs to pull in several volunteers before every service. The baptistry unfolds and sets up in **under 2 minutes**, with no tools, by a single team member. That's a direct benefit on Sundays when the tech team already has enough on its plate.

## Stability that doesn't depend on air pressure

Unlike an inflatable structure, the Kollder baptistry's stability comes from a stainless steel frame with 6 support points and cross-bracing. It stays the same on flat ground or slightly uneven flooring (a tent, outdoors), with no risk of sagging during immersion.

## Dimensions built for full immersion

205 × 80 × 60 cm (81 × 31 × 24 in) set up: enough space for an adult lying down, with the officiant able to assist from either side of the pool. These proportions avoid the common compromise of an undersized pool that makes the moment hard to carry out with dignity.

## A material built for repeated use

Food-grade stainless steel cleans and disinfects quickly between services, with no degradation over time. Unlike a PVC coating, it doesn't lose its seal through repeated folding and unfolding cycles, extending the equipment's lifespan across many years.

## Compact storage between baptisms

Folded into a carry bag roughly 85 × 10 × 10 cm (33 × 4 × 4 in) at about 11 kg (24 lb), the baptistry stores easily in a closet, a storage room, or a car trunk, without taking up much space.
